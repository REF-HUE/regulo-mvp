Cape Town Zone Lookup

Enter your property's zoning details from your title deed or City of Cape Town records to generate a full zoning analysis.

Where to find your zone: Check your title deed, the City of Cape Town GIS viewer, or ask your architect/town planner. The zone is listed on your municipal zoning certificate.

Cape Town uses a Floor Factor system:

Floor Factor works like FAR (Floor Area Ratio) — it determines your maximum buildable floor space relative to erf size. Some zones (like SR1) have parameters that vary by erf size, which Regulo handles automatically.
