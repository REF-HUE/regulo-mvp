Johannesburg Zone Lookup

Enter your property's zoning details from your title deed or City of Johannesburg records to generate a full zoning analysis.

Where to find your zone: Check your title deed, the City of Johannesburg GIS portal, or ask your architect/town planner. The zone and height zone are listed on your municipal rates account and zoning certificate.

Johannesburg uses a Height Zone system:

  • Height Zone A — Low intensity (suburban areas)
  • Height Zone B — Medium intensity (transitional areas)
  • Height Zone C — High intensity (urban core & corridors)

The height zone affects your FAR, coverage, and height allowances within the same zone category.
